Guide

Phone and PC on your own Wi-Fi — screen, camera, files and messages, with no account and no cloud.

Getting started

DuoLink connects an Android phone to this PC over your own Wi-Fi. Everything — screen, files, messages, clipboard — travels directly between the two devices. There is no account and no cloud.

1

Put both devices on one network

Join the same Wi-Fi on the phone and the PC. Guest networks and public hotspots usually block device-to-device traffic; a home network or the phone's hotspot works.

2

Scan the pairing QR

Open Overview on the PC and the Pair tab in the Android app. Point the phone at the QR code. The code is single-use and expires after three minutes.

3

Confirm the six-digit code

The same number appears on both screens. If it matches, approve on the PC. The phone is now remembered and reconnects automatically on the same network.

The certificate fingerprint is pinned on both sides. A device impersonating the PC on your network cannot complete a reconnect.

Screen mirroring

The phone publishes its screen as a live JPEG stream; this PC renders the newest frame it has. The picture follows the phone's rotation, so a landscape game fills a landscape window without reconnecting.

Start a stream

  1. Open Phone Screen and press Start stream.
  2. Accept Android's screen-share prompt on the handset. On Android 14+ choose entire screen.
  3. For remote taps and typing, enable the DuoLink Accessibility Service in Android settings.

Use the Back, Home and Recents buttons in the side panel for navigation, and Type on phone to send the PC keyboard into the phone's active field.

Orientation and window modes

Rotation is detected from the frames themselves. Portrait frames get a tall window; landscape frames get a wide one, and Pop out gives the phone its own resizable window that stays in step with rotation. Fullscreen hides the rest of the app; press Esc to return.

Getting a smoother picture

  • Use the 5 GHz band when the router offers it. 2.4 GHz is shared and adds jitter.
  • Keep the phone within line of sight; a wall or a microwave between phone and router shows up as dropped frames.
  • The stream adapts on its own: when the link backs up it lowers JPEG quality, then resolution, then frame rate, and climbs back when the link clears. The status bar shows the picture size and real frame rate it is achieving.
  • Close battery savers that throttle background work on the phone; the app already holds a wake lock while mirroring.

Phone as a webcam

The phone camera is published as an MJPEG stream on this PC. OBS (or any app that accepts a network video URL) can display it, and OBS Virtual Camera exposes it to Zoom, Teams and Meet.

  1. Open Webcam, pick the camera, resolution, frame rate and JPEG quality.
  2. Press Start webcam and allow camera access on the phone.
  3. In OBS: Sources → + → Media Source → uncheck Local File → paste the URL.
  4. Start OBS Virtual Camera, then select it as the camera in your meeting app.

DuoLink streams up to 1080p at 60 fps. Use 60 fps for motion (handheld walk-throughs, demonstrations) and 30 fps for stationary shots; if the preview stutters, drop resolution before quality — pixels cost far more bandwidth than JPEG quality.

Chat, clipboard and files

Messages and files travel over the authenticated session only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

From the PC

  • Chat & Files — type a message, or drag files onto the drop area to send them to the phone's Downloads/DuoLink folder.
  • Push PC clipboard sends the current clipboard text; auto-sync is a switch in the same panel.
  • Transfers show live progress and stay listed in the transfer history. A failed transfer is removed rather than left half-written.

Browsing PC files from the phone

On the phone, open Share → PC Files. You get the folders the PC has approved, with breadcrumbs, file size and type, and buttons to download to the phone, upload from the phone, create a folder, rename, delete and refresh.

  • Every local drive (C:, D:, removable disks) is reachable while Settings → PC file access is on. Turn it off and the phone's browser closes immediately.
  • Windows permissions are respected: protected system folders, files held open by another program and folders your Windows account cannot read are reported, never bypassed.
  • Paths are resolved and checked on the PC side, so .. sequences cannot escape the authorized drives.
  • Delete asks on the phone first and moves the item to the Windows Recycle Bin unless you explicitly pick permanent delete.

Media player

Settings

Appearance

DuoLink ships with two themes: Dark and Light. Both are the same interface with a different surface and accent palette, so nothing moves when you switch. The choice is stored on this PC. Follow Windows appearance hands the decision to Windows, and Reduce motion turns off page transitions.

Connection behaviour

  • Start with Windows and Minimise to tray decide how the app behaves when you sign in or close the window.
  • Trusted Devices lists every phone that has paired; revoking one removes its session secret immediately.
  • The kill switch (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q) disables phone-to-PC input everywhere, including the Overview switch.

PC file access

One switch authorizes the phone's file browser for the whole PC. It can then browse every drive, open folders, search, download, upload, rename, copy, move and delete. Deleting needs confirmation on the phone; the item goes to the Recycle Bin unless permanent delete is chosen. Files the phone sends without a destination arrive in the received-files folder shown above.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Phone cannot find the PCCheck both devices are on the same SSID, not a guest network. Allow DuoLink through Windows Firewall on private networks (TCP 3000).
QR code expiredTokens last three minutes. Press Refresh QR on the Overview page and scan again.
Wrong PC addressThe app skips VPN, WSL and Hyper-V adapters. If more than one physical adapter exists, set LAN_IP before launch to pin the address.
Certificate error after reinstallA reinstall generates a new self-signed certificate. Forget the PC on the phone and pair again.
Mirror is blackBanking, password and DRM apps set FLAG_SECURE; Android returns black for them by design. Otherwise re-grant screen capture.
Mirror stuttersMove closer to the router, prefer 5 GHz, close the phone's battery saver, and stop other large transfers during a game.
Taps do nothingEnable the DuoLink Accessibility Service on the phone; the mirror itself keeps working without it.
Phone drops in backgroundSet DuoLink to unrestricted battery use (Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo and Vivo all hide this under different names) and allow autostart.
Phone shows no drivesTurn on Settings → PC file access on the PC. If it is on and still empty, the phone is not connected: check the connection pill at the bottom of the sidebar.

The diagnostic log lives in the app data folder; Settings → Open log opens it, and Run diagnostics reports phone permissions, service state and latency.
